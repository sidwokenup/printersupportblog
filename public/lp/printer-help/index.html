Logic Board / Power Surge Burnout $140 - $230 (mainboard swap) $119 - $189 (equivalent model) Replace — Circuit-level faults frequently recur and exceed residual machine value.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear troubleshooting answers for common printer errors and setup questions.

Why does my printer say offline when it is powered on and connected? ▼

Printers frequently report an offline status due to stale network socket bindings, sleeping Wi-Fi adapters, or Windows setting the queue to 'Use Printer Offline'. To resolve this, open Windows Printers & Scanners, click your printer queue, uncheck 'Use Printer Offline', and restart your wireless router and printer simultaneously.

What causes a printer to print completely blank pages? ▼

Blank pages typically occur when protective shipping tape remains on printhead nozzles, ink cartridges are depleted without reporting an empty sensor state, printhead nozzles are severely clogged with dried pigment, or the transfer roller has lost electrical contact.

How do I fix a ghost paper jam when there is no paper stuck inside? ▼

A ghost paper jam occurs when optical paper sensors are obscured by paper dust, toner powder, or microscopic scraps. Clean the internal feed rollers with a lint-free cloth lightly dampened with distilled water, inspect the duplexer tray with a flashlight, and gently wipe the sensor optical flags.

Why are my print jobs stuck deleting in the Windows print queue? ▼

Jobs get stuck in deleting status when the Windows Print Spooler service crashes while communicating with the printer driver. To clear it, open an administrative command prompt, run 'net stop spooler', delete all files in C:\Windows\System32\spool\PRINTERS, and restart the service with 'net start spooler'.

Why does my printer disconnect from Wi-Fi whenever the router reboots? ▼

Most modern wireless routers utilize dynamic IP allocation (DHCP) that assigns a new local IP address upon rebooting, breaking existing operating system port configurations. Assigning a permanent Static IP address or DHCP reservation in your router admin console prevents this disconnect.

What is the difference between 2.4GHz and 5GHz Wi-Fi for printers? ▼

Nearly all consumer wireless printers only feature 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network cards. If your home network uses a single combined SSID for both 2.4GHz and 5GHz, the printer may fail to connect. Separating the network frequencies or enabling guest 2.4GHz Wi-Fi guarantees reliable wireless communication.

How do I fix streaky, faded, or missing colors on my prints? ▼

Streaking and color fading indicate dried ink blocking thermal or piezoelectric nozzles. Run two consecutive automated printhead cleaning cycles through your printer software utilities, followed by a nozzle check test page. If streaks persist, perform a manual printhead purge or replace worn toner drums.

Why did my printer stop working immediately after a Windows update? ▼

Major operating system updates often overwrite proprietary vendor printer drivers with generic Microsoft WSD (Web Services for Devices) drivers, causing spooler timeouts. Remove the printer device from your system settings and reinstall the official manufacturer full software suite.

What does 'Printer in Error State' mean on Windows 10 and 11? ▼

'Printer in Error State' indicates an unhandled hardware or communication exception, such as an open maintenance hatch, an unseated ink cartridge, exhausted paper trays, or corrupted USB communication ports. Performing a complete 60-second power drain typically resets the internal logic board.

What should I do if my printer carriage is stuck or jammed? ▼

Turn off the printer and disconnect the power cable. Open the main cartridge access door and inspect the carriage rail for torn paper scraps, dislodged clips, or packing tape. Gently slide the print carriage by hand from side to side to verify smooth movement before repowering.

How do I fix a printer that refuses to print black ink even with a full cartridge? ▼

When black ink fails while color prints normally, the black printhead nozzles are clogged or the cartridge vent hole is sealed. Ensure the yellow/orange pull tab is completely removed from the top air vent, and perform an ink system priming cycle or deep nozzle purge.

Is it cheaper to repair a broken printer or replace it? ▼

If your inkjet printer requires a printhead replacement or logic board repair exceeding $150, purchasing a new modern unit is almost always more economical. However, minor feed roller replacements or waste ink pad cleanings are inexpensive fixes that extend machine life substantially.

How do I locate the exact serial number and model on my printer? ▼

Printer serial numbers and exact model numbers are located on a manufacturer barcode sticker affixed to the back, underneath the scanner lid, or inside the front cartridge access bay. This alphanumeric code is required to identify authentic warranty coverage and driver downloads.

What causes the dreaded Canon B200 error code? ▼

The Canon B200 error code signifies a printhead voltage abnormality or thermal overheating. Turn off the printer, unplug the power cord for 30 minutes, remove and reseat all ink cartridges, and inspect the printhead contacts. If the error persists, the printhead assembly has suffered hardware failure.

Why won't my laptop find my wireless printer during initial setup? ▼

Laptops fail to discover network printers if the laptop is connected to a 5GHz band while the printer resides on 2.4GHz, if 'Network Discovery' is disabled in Windows Network & Sharing Center, or if third-party firewall software blocks mDNS / Bonjour broadcast packets.

How can I print from my smartphone without installing complex software? ▼

Apple devices can print immediately via native AirPrint when connected to the same local Wi-Fi network. Android devices utilize the Mopria Print Service or Default Print Service built into the operating system settings under Connected Devices > Printing.

What is an ink absorber pad error and can I bypass it? ▼

Inkjet printers direct waste ink from cleaning cycles into internal sponge pads. Once internal page count counters calculate the pads are saturated, the printer halts to prevent ink leakage onto your desk. Resetting requires maintenance software and replacing the internal absorbent felt.

How do I safely update my printer firmware without bricking the device? ▼

Firmware updates should be performed while the printer is connected directly via USB or over an exceptionally stable Wi-Fi connection. Never power off or unplug the printer while firmware installation is progressing on the screen, as interruption corrupts onboard flash memory.
