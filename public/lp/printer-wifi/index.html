Frequently Asked Questions

Clear troubleshooting answers for common printer errors and setup questions.

Why won't my printer connect to my 5GHz Wi-Fi network? ▼

Most consumer and small-office printers contain network chipsets designed strictly for 2.4GHz Wi-Fi frequencies. If your router broadcasts only on 5GHz or merges both frequencies under one name, the printer cannot discover the SSID. Splitting your router bands allows smooth 2.4GHz connection.

What does a blinking Wi-Fi light on my printer mean? ▼

A blinking blue or amber Wi-Fi LED indicates that the printer is broadcasting a connection request or attempting to authenticate with an access point. If it blinks continuously without going solid, authentication failed due to an incorrect Wi-Fi password or MAC filtering rule.

Why does my printer drop off the network after changing my router? ▼

Replacing or rebooting your router resets the local IP subnet and changes the network security protocol (such as WPA3). You must run the wireless setup wizard on the printer display or via USB to enter the new network credentials.

How do I find my printer's IP address? ▼

Print a Network Configuration Page from the printer's onboard Setup or Wireless menu. Alternatively, access your wireless router's administration portal to view connected DHCP client devices and their assigned IPv4 addresses.
