# Google Ads Campaign Specifications: Printer_Help_USA_Search

## 1. Campaign Structure & Parameters
- **Campaign Name**: `Printer_Help_USA_Search`
- **Goal / Objective**: Leads / Conversions
- **Campaign Type**: Search Network
- **Networks**:
  - Google Search Network: **Enabled**
  - Search Partners: **Disabled** (protect Quality Score and prevent bot traffic)
  - Google Display Network: **Disabled** (strictly text search)

## 2. Budget & Bidding Strategy
- **Daily Budget**: $25.00 / day starting testing budget ($750/month)
- **Bidding Strategy**: **Maximize Conversions**
- **Maximum CPC Cap**: $1.20
- **Post-Learning Target**: Once 30 verified conversions are logged in GA4/Ads, switch bidding strategy to **Target CPA** at **$8.00 - $12.00**.

## 3. Geographic Targeting & Languages
- **Targeted Location**: **United States** only
- **Target Option**: *Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations* (Do NOT select "Presence or Interest")
- **Excluded Locations**: All other countries and territories
- **Languages**: **English**

## 4. Devices & Bid Adjustments
- **Mobile Phones**: **+20% bid adjustment** (high-intent, immediate chat initiators)
- **Computers (Desktop)**: **0% (baseline)**
- **Tablets**: **-20% bid adjustment**

## 5. Ad Schedule & Delivery
- **Schedule**: **24 hours a day, 7 days a week (Monday - Sunday)**
  - Chat service operates 24/7.
- **Ad Rotation**: **Optimize: Prefer best performing ads**
